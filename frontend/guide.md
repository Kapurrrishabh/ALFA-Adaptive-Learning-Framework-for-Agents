### What this app does

StockIntel helps you study stocks on India's NSE and make up your own mind.
For each stock it shows a call, the reasons for and against it, and how well each method did in tests.

It is decision support, not investment advice. It does not know your whole money picture, and it can be wrong.
Check the figures against the company's NSE or BSE filings before you buy or sell.
The app never places trades. You buy and sell in your own broker app.

### Signing in

You can sign in with your Google account, or with an email address and a password. Sign in the same way each time.
- With Google, you pick your account on Google's page and come straight back.
- With email, we send you a link to confirm your address. Open it before you sign in.
- Choose a long password. A short sentence is easy to remember. Do not put the first part of your email in it.
- If you forget your password, ask for a new one by email. A new password signs you out on every device.
To sign out, open the Account menu and choose Sign out. This also clears your chat and your saved choices from this device, so the next person starts fresh.

### Choosing your style and risk

You choose a style and a risk level each time you build a basket or a plan.
- **Momentum** picks the stocks that rose the most over recent months. It is the one method here with a measured edge.
- **Low volatility** picks the stocks whose price swings the least.
- **Blend** mixes the two.
- **Balanced** risk puts less money in when the market is in a state where momentum stocks have crashed before.
- **Defensive** risk holds cash when the market trend is down, and puts less in when prices swing a lot.
- **Always invested** puts all the money in. The Plan page calls it Full exposure.

### Adding what you own

The app can only track and plan for the stocks it knows you own. There are two ways to add them.
- **By hand.** Open [Holdings](#/portfolio) and press Add transaction, or press Add to portfolio on any stock page. Enter the stock, buy or sell, the date, how many shares, the price per share and any fees.
- **From a basket.** On [Build](#/builder), pick an amount, a style and a risk level, and the app picks the stocks and the share counts. Buy them in your broker app first. Then press Add all to my portfolio, which adds them as buys dated today.
To fix a mistake, delete the trade from the Transactions list on Holdings and add it again.

### Reading a stock page

Type a stock's name or symbol in the search box at the top to open its page.
- **The top card** shows the price, today's change, and quick facts such as past returns, the year's high and low, and the P/E ratio.
- **The chart** has three tabs. Technical shows daily candles, moving averages, patterns and forecast ranges. Intraday shows the price in short steps over the last few days. Price & projection shows the price line and the range ahead.
- **Should I buy?** gives the app's call, the reasons you might buy, and the reasons you might not.
- **Analyst note** is a short write-up of the models' numbers. It says whether an AI model worded it.
- **The tabs at the bottom** hold the company overview, the research from every engine, recent news, and trades by the company's own promoters and directors.
- **Research report** writes a long report you can keep. Find it later in [My reports](#/reports).
A forecast range shows where the price may land. It is not a promise.
Chart patterns are drawn so you can see them, but tests found that most of them do not predict the price. [Track record](#/performance) shows every test.

### What BUY, WAIT, DON'T BUY and HOLD mean

The call on a stock page comes from one tested rule: momentum. The app ranks a list of large NSE companies by how much they rose over recent months, and the stock page shows the rank.
- **BUY** means the stock is near the top of the ranking, so it belongs in a momentum portfolio. When the market is in a risky state for momentum, the call is BUY — HALF SIZE.
- **WAIT** means the rank is good enough to keep the stock if you own it, but not good enough to buy it fresh.
- **DON'T BUY** means the stock's momentum is weak.
- **HOLD** is what WAIT says when you already own the stock. In the same way, BUY becomes HOLD / ADD, and DON'T BUY becomes SELL AT REBALANCE, which means sell on the next review date.
- **NO SIGNAL** means the stock is not in the ranked list, so the tested rule does not cover it.
The reasons for and against come from the other engines, such as trend, company results, risk and news. They help you judge the call, but they do not change it.

### Your plan and stop-losses

[Plan](#/signals) looks at what you own and any new money you add. It tells you what to sell, trim, buy, add to and hold, with share counts and the reasons.
Enter the new money, pick a style and a risk level, and press Build plan. The plan shows the next review date. Build it again then.

A stop-loss is a price below today's price. If the stock falls to it, you sell, so one bad stock cannot cost you too much. The plan and the stock page show a stop-loss for the momentum stocks they suggest.
The stop-loss is the app's own safety rule. In tests it made the worst falls smaller, but it also cut the return.

When a sale has a tax cost, the plan adds a tax note. Read the How much to trust this card at the end of the plan before you act.

### Asking questions

On [Ask](#/chat), type a question in your own words, such as "Should I buy TCS?", "What is RSI?" or "Compare INFY and HCLTECH".
The chat remembers the conversation, so you can follow up with "Why?" or "Compare it with HUL". Press New chat to start over.

You can also ask about one stock in the box on its page, and read short lessons in [Lessons](#/learn).
When the self-learning agent is running, you can tick a box to get its view as well. It stays quiet when it is not sure.

### Your daily AI answers

Some parts of the app use AI models, such as the analyst note on a stock page and the self-learning agent. They say when an AI model wrote the words.
Each account can use these models only so many times a day, and the whole app has a daily limit too.
When a limit is reached, the app says so and tells you when more AI answers come. The analyst note and the chat still answer from the app's own numbers.

### Your data and how to delete it

The app keeps your email address and your portfolio: the trades you add, your plans and your reports. Each account sees only its own portfolio.
The Privacy page, in the Account menu, lists everything that is kept and where.

To delete it all, open the Account menu and choose Delete my account. This deletes your account and everything tied to it, and it cannot be undone.
