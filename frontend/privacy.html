Your data in StockIntel

What the app keeps about you

  • Your account: your email address and whether you have confirmed it. If you sign in with a password, the app keeps a scrambled form of it that cannot be turned back into the password. If you sign in with Google, it keeps Google's ID for your account, but not Google's keys to your account.
  • Your sign-ins: one record for each device you are signed in on. It ends when you sign out, or when it runs out.
  • Your portfolio: your cash, risk level and time horizon; your transactions (stock, buy or sell, quantity, price, fees, date and sector); the portfolio values the app records; the reports you make; your buy and sell plans; and the alerts you dismiss.
  • Your daily count of AI answers, so the app can keep to the daily limit.
  • Your chat: while you chat, the app remembers the conversation so that follow-up questions work. It is kept in the server's memory only, not saved, and is gone when the server restarts.
  • Server logs: the server writes down the address each request came from, what it asked for and when.

Questions you send to the self-learning agent ("Also ask my self-learning agent") are kept by the agent, with its answers, so it can learn which answers were right. They are kept under the app's name, not yours, and they stay when you delete your account.

Who can see it

You, when you are signed in, and the person who runs this copy of the app.

The backup

So that a restart does not lose anything, the app copies its database to a private Hugging Face dataset every few minutes. The copy holds the accounts and portfolios, but no sign-ins. Only the person who runs the app can read it.

How to delete your data

While signed in, open the account menu and choose Delete my account. This removes your account, your sign-ins, your portfolio and everything listed under it, and your counts of AI answers, at once. The next backup no longer has them. Older versions of the backup still do until the person who runs the app removes them.
