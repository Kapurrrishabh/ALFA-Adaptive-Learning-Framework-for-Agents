# Indian equity market essentials

## Exchanges and indices
India's two main stock exchanges are the National Stock Exchange (NSE) and BSE
(formerly Bombay Stock Exchange). The NIFTY 50 is NSE's benchmark index of 50
large companies; the S&P BSE Sensex tracks 30. Bank Nifty tracks the most liquid
banking stocks. Sector indices (Nifty IT, Nifty Pharma, Nifty FMCG and others)
track industries. The markets regulator is SEBI.

## Trading hours and settlement
Regular equity trading runs from 9:15 am to 3:30 pm IST, Monday to Friday, with a
pre-open session from 9:00 am. Equity trades settle T+1 (one working day after the
trade). Exchanges publish a holiday calendar each year; always check the current
year's list.

## Price bands and circuit breakers
Individual stocks can have daily price bands (for example 2%, 5%, 10% or 20%)
beyond which they cannot trade that day; many large, derivative-eligible stocks
have no fixed band but dynamic limits. Market-wide circuit breakers halt trading
if the NIFTY 50 or Sensex moves 10%, 15% or 20% in a day. Surveillance frameworks
(ASM and GSM) can impose extra restrictions on specific stocks.

## Transaction costs
Costs include brokerage, Securities Transaction Tax (STT), exchange transaction
charges, SEBI fees, stamp duty and GST on brokerage. STT on delivery equity trades
is 0.1% on both buy and sell. Round-trip costs for delivery trades through a
discount broker are typically around 0.1–0.15% of trade value; intraday and
frequent trading cost more as a share of returns. Verify current rates with your
broker.

## Taxes on listed equity (verify current rules)
From 23 July 2024, long-term capital gains (shares held more than 12 months) on
listed equity with STT paid are taxed at 12.5% on gains above ₹1.25 lakh per
financial year, and short-term capital gains at 20%. Dividends are taxed at the
investor's slab rate. Tax rules change with Union Budgets; confirm the rules for
the relevant financial year before acting.

## Corporate disclosures
Listed companies must disclose material events and results to the exchanges under
SEBI's LODR regulations; these filings on NSE and BSE are primary sources and more
reliable than press coverage. Quarterly results are due within 45 days of quarter
end.
